Welcome to IbukaPay

A distributed Java application for organizing expenses and avoiding late payments through automated reminders.

Members: register people who manage their payments, and keep their contact details so reminders can be delivered on time.
Expenses: record each payment obligation with an amount and a due date. Any expense that is still pending past its due date is flagged as overdue.
Automated reminders: when a pending expense is saved, a reminder is automatically scheduled for the member before the due date.

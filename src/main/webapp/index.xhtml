Welcome to IbukaPay

A personal financial obligation and bill tracking system for recording the money you owe, money owed to you, and your household bills - with due dates, statuses and reminders.

Contacts: save any person (a friend, lender, or lender-to-you) without them needing an account, then link them to your financial records.
Financial records: record money you owe, money owed to you, or bills - with amount, remaining amount, due date and status. Anything still pending past its due date is flagged as overdue.
Automated reminders: when a pending record is saved, a reminder is automatically scheduled before the due date.
